Privacy Policy

Last updated 2026-10-14

This policy covers everything published by Honest Tools: the Honest Guide website, our Chrome extensions, the products we sell on Gumroad, and our YouTube upload app, Honest Tools Uploader. In short: we do not collect personal data, and we do not sell or share any.

The Honest Guide website

  • This is a static website hosted on GitHub Pages. We do not run analytics, ads or tracking scripts, and the site itself does not set cookies.
  • GitHub may log technical information, such as IP addresses, when it serves pages. See GitHub's privacy statement.
  • Some free tools on this site remember your inputs or checkboxes in your own browser (localStorage). That data stays on your device and is never sent to us.
  • Some Japanese pages contain Rakuten Affiliate links. If you click one, Rakuten may use cookies to record the purchase. Links to other services (Rakuten, Gumroad, the Chrome Web Store, BOOTH, GitHub, YouTube) take you to sites that have their own privacy policies.

Our Chrome extensions

  • Honest Headers uses only the declarativeNetRequest, storage and alarms permissions. It does not read the content of the pages you visit and makes no network requests of its own. It collects no data. Your settings are stored only in your own browser (chrome.storage).
  • Honest Cookies reads and changes cookies only in your own browser. It makes no network requests of its own, collects no data, and never sends cookies to us or anyone else.
  • Honest Scraper reads a page only when you click its button or menu item, and only in that tab. What it reads stays on your computer unless you copy or save it. It makes no network requests of its own and collects no data.

None of our extensions use analytics, telemetry, advertising or remotely hosted code.

Purchases on Gumroad

Our paid products (the Honest Headers Pro key, config templates and checklists) are sold through Gumroad. Gumroad handles payment and your email address under its own privacy policy. We do not receive or store your card details.

YouTube: Honest Tools Uploader

Honest Tools Uploader is our own app. It uses YouTube API Services only to upload our own videos and captions to our own YouTube channel, Honest Tools (@honesttoolsdev). It does not access, collect, store or share data about any other YouTube user. The authorization token is stored only on our own computer and is never shared.

  • By using YouTube features, you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service.
  • Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.
  • You can revoke an app's access to your Google account at any time on the Google security settings page.

Children

We do not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

We may update this policy. The current version, with its date, is always on this page.
